Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Michi uses anonymous sign-in so you can start using the app without creating a login first.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Profile screen in Michi and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Profile screen in Michi and tap Delete Account to remove your account and associated data.

What is the free decision?

You can try Michi with one free decision. Unlimited decisions are available with the one-time Pro purchase.
